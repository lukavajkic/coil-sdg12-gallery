01 / OUR STARTING POINT

IT'S NOT JUST
ABOUT BINS.

12 UN SUSTAINABLE
DEVELOPMENT GOAL

Our choices shape what happens next.

In Slovenia, waste collection has changed enormously. But separating waste, recycling materials and reusing a working device are three different things. This exhibition shows the difference, using published statistics and examples from our own region.

Our focus is SDG 12 — responsible consumption and production — and the electronics we use every day.

04 / OUR HOME, OUR EXAMPLES

ON THE GROUND
IN NOVA GORICA.

Local infrastructure is what turns environmental goals into everyday choices. Here are the places and practices we want to document ourselves.

REAL LOCAL INITIATIVE / 20 APRIL 2022

Giving working electronics another chance.

ZEOS and Komunala Nova Gorica opened a reuse corner at the CERO Nova Gorica collection centre for electrical and electronic appliances that still work. [5]

Read the city's announcement ↗

What is accepted where?

In the Nova Gorica area, Komunala describes collection centres, ecological islands and small blue battery compartments on updated street containers. Not every street bin accepts every electrical item. Always check the label and the current operator's instructions. [6]
